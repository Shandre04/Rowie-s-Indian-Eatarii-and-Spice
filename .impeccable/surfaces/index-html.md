# Home (index.html), plus menu.html and spices.html

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Southern Suburbs phone users from Facebook or Google Maps on slow 4G. Job: WhatsApp order in two taps; second, trust the spice shop enough to walk in. Proof on hand: halaal, 4.5 stars from 10 reviews, real address and number. Everything else is a visible placeholder.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop's own painted sign and order docket, not a restaurant template. It refuses the photo-hero-plus-three-cards layout.

OWN-WORLD: Chilli-red signboard field owns the top and the footer; cream counter paper below. Turmeric is the one sharp accent (the order button on red). Cumin-brown text. Cardamom green only for "open". Kitchen docket for today's special (perforated edge, handwritten dish name), jar labels for spices with drawn chilli heat marks, a price-board menu with dotted leaders. Hand-drawn SVG chilli and spice marks, faint spice-dust texture.

STORY: In one glance: Durban-style curry, made in Plumstead, order on WhatsApp, collect in shop, halaal, open or closed now. Regulars tap the order or the special. Newcomers scroll: how it works, menu, spices, find us, FAQ.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width chilli-red sign: big cream display headline, turmeric "Order on WhatsApp" button under it at thumb height, trust line and live open/closed chip under that. On phones the top of the special docket peeks at the bottom edge.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "Think handwritten shop sign and jar labels", "a ticket or receipt motif for 'your order'", "Hero. One line saying what it is and where. … Next to it, the primary button: Order on WhatsApp."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
